This Privacy Policy explains how the puzzle game "Mixly Color Sort" (package: com.biko4.mixlycolorsort), developed by BikoApps, handles information.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive any personal information from you. We do not run our own servers and we do not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Your player name, chosen avatar, game progress, coins, unlocked themes and settings (such as sound and language) are saved only on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. This is handled by Google according to its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time from your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact us

Email: biko.dev.app@gmail.com
